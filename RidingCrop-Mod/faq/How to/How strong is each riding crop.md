How strong is each riding crop?

Blunt damage grows by 40% per tier and the bleed by 25% per tier. At quality 1 and skill 0 the crops deal 20, 28, 39.2, 54.9, 76.8, 107.6 and 150.6 blunt damage, from Do I boar you? up to Seal the deal!. Durability goes from 200 to 500 in steps of 50. All stats are fixed by the mod, so everyone on a server has the same values.
