Which crafting station does each riding crop need?

Do I boar you? and Dear, oh deer! are made at the workbench (levels 1 and 2). Bear with me!, Howl yeah! and Loxy Poxy are made at the forge (levels 1, 2 and 3). Askvin worthy and Seal the deal! are made at the black forge (levels 1 and 2). Each upgrade needs one station level more than the quality before it, at the same station.
