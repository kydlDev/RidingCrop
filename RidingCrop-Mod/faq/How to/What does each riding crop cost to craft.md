What does each riding crop cost to craft?

Every crop takes 10 hides or pelts, 2 of a rarer material and 5 pieces of wood. Do I boar you?: 10 Leather Scraps, 2 Resin, 5 Wood. Dear, oh deer!: 10 Deer Hide, 2 Bronze, 5 Fine Wood. Bear with me!: 10 Bear Hide, 2 Surtling Core, 5 Core Wood. Howl yeah!: 10 Wolf Pelt, 2 Obsidian, 5 Fine Wood. Loxy Poxy: 10 Lox Pelt, 2 Tar, 5 Ancient Bark. Askvin worthy: 10 Askvin Hide, 2 Charred Bone, 5 Ashwood. Seal the deal!: 10 Moose Hide, 2 Seal Pelt, 5 Timberwood.
