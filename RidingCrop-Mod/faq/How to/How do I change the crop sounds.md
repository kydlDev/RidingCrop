How do I change the crop sounds?

Open the KY hub (F6), pick Riding Crop and use the Sound settings: `Enable` turns the own sounds on or off, `WhooshVolume` and `SlapVolume` set the volume (0 to 1), `ReplaceVanillaSounds` decides whether the Club's sounds also play, and `SlapNormalize` cuts the quiet build-up from the start of the hit sound. They need a game restart. A swing that hits several targets plays the slap only once.
