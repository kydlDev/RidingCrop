How do I turn on the detailed log?

Set `[DebugLog] Enable` to `true` in the KY hub (F6) or in `BepInEx/config/com.kydldev.RidingCrop.cfg`. It works while the game runs. The log (`BepInEx/LogOutput.log`) then shows the asset bundle contents, each crop's stats, recipe and station, the sound setup, and every whoosh, slap and bleed event.
